# Orbity privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Orbity is a desktop pet app for Windows and Mac. This page explains what information Orbity uses and where it goes. The short version: **Orbity has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and nothing is sold or shared.**

## What stays on your computer

Orbity saves these on your computer only, in its own settings folder:

- your pet's name, look and personality, and your app settings;
- the name you gave yourself and the notes you wrote under "About you";
- things you taught your pet (treats and corrections), game scores, reminders and friendship progress;
- API keys you add, stored encrypted using your computer's own protection (Windows or macOS).

Your chats are not saved to disk; they disappear when you quit Orbity. You can see, delete or reset what your pet has learned in Customize, and you can delete everything by uninstalling Orbity and removing its settings folder (see "Uninstall" in the [README](README.md)).

## Where your messages go

Orbity needs an AI "brain" to answer you. What happens depends on the brain you choose:

- **Free brain (Ollama):** your messages go to the Ollama program running on **your own computer**. They do not leave it.
- **Premium brains (your own API key):** your messages are sent over the internet to the company you chose: for example Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (ChatGPT), Google (Gemini), or another service whose address you entered. With each question Orbity also sends the recent conversation, your pet's name and personality, and your "About you" notes, so answers fit you. That company handles the data under its own privacy terms. Your API key is sent only to that company.

Orbity does not send your messages anywhere else, and it does not read, copy or keep a log of them.

## Screenshots

Orbity takes a screenshot **only when you press a button** for it (the 👀 button, "Use my screen" on the whiteboard, or when you say yes after asking it to look). It captures one screen, once. With the Free brain it stays on your computer; with a Premium brain it is sent to the company you chose, along with your question. Orbity does not save screenshots to disk. Orbity never looks at your screen on its own.

## Update checks (GitHub)

The Windows installer version and the Mac version **check GitHub for updates**: when Orbity starts, and then about every six hours, it asks GitHub's public releases page whether a newer version exists. On Windows the installer version downloads the update from GitHub too. GitHub, like any website, can see your internet address and the kind of computer you use when this happens, and GitHub's own privacy statement applies to that. Orbity sends nothing about you, your chats or your pet. The Microsoft Store version does not do this; the Microsoft Store updates it.

## AI answers

Answers are written by AI and can be wrong or, rarely, not suitable. Orbity filters what your pet is allowed to change about itself to keep it family-friendly. If you see a reply that is wrong or inappropriate, please report it by email (see "Contact").

## Children

Orbity is a general-audience app and is not aimed at children under 13. Orbity does not knowingly collect information from children. A child using Orbity with a Premium brain should have a parent set up the account and key.

## Your choices

You can switch to the Free brain at any time so nothing leaves your computer, delete what your pet learned, remove your "About you" notes, remove your API keys, or uninstall Orbity.

## Contact

Questions, problems or reports about an unsuitable reply: email **orbitypets@gmail.com**. Choosing "Report a problem" in Orbity opens a new email for you to review; only what you write and send reaches us, and we use it only to look into your report and reply. For general bug reports and ideas you can also use the [GitHub issues page](https://github.com/VSINH123/orbitee-releases/issues/new), but that page is public, so please don't put chats or personal details there.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
